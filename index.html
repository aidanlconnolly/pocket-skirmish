How to playField Manual

Goal: capture the enemy HQ (move a Rifleman/Scout onto it, choose Capture twice), or destroy every enemy unit.

Your turn: tap a unit → blue tiles show where it can move → tap to move → choose Attack, Capture, or Wait. Tap your own Factory to build units with gold.

Fog of war: you only see what your units can see. Scouts and choppers have the longest sight — use them to spot ambushes.

Transport: the APC carries two infantry. Move infantry onto it to load; select the APC and Unload to drop them at the front.

Combat: damage scales with the attacker's health and the defender's cover. Forests and mountains protect. Most units strike back — but not artillery, and not if you out-range them.
